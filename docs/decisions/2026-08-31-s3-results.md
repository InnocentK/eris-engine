**S3 did not answer its question.** `eval/score.py` sets
`comparison.blocked: true`: the three arms share exactly **one** cell
(`control-clean-io#0`), so the 3b-3 and 4-3 paired deltas - the entire reason
this campaign exists - cannot be computed. It cost **$18.21 committed** to learn
that. No arm difference below may be quoted as a comparison.

## Why the comparison collapsed: three causes, none of them the panel

The claude+grok panel worked exactly as intended. Proposals cached within each
task, so `indep_verify_iter` paid only for judging (`$0.70` per control cell
against `indep_verify`'s `$1.28`), and every dollar was attributable. The
pairing was not the problem.

1. **The `$1` per-worker call cap killed arm 4's entire plan data.** Both
   `indep_verify_critique` plan cells stopped with
   `claude: subtype='error_max_budget_usd' terminal_reason='budget_exhausted':
   Reached maximum budget ($1)` - one on a CRITIC call, one on the ADJUDICATOR.
   Eris classified it `budget_tripped`, aborted the step, skipped `verify2` and
   `adjudicate`, and marked the cells incomplete. Arm 4 therefore has **no plan
   cells at all**, and a 4-3 delta on the only category with planted findings is
   impossible.
2. **A validator error dropped one `indep_verify` control cell.**
   `DISSENT_EMPTY_WITH_CONTEST` on `control-guarded-r0-indep_verify`: a contest
   exists and `dissent` is empty. `validate.py` already says this "would fire on
   nearly every arm-4 cell today because `_contest_exists` counts any
   non-REFUTED challenge and nothing ever refutes one", with the reasons in
   `2026-08-30-deferred-validator-decisions.md`. A known, deferred, deliberate
   behaviour - which nobody costed into a campaign design.
3. **Together they leave one shared cell.** `indep_verify` included 3 of 4,
   `indep_verify_critique` 2 of 4, `indep_verify_iter` 4 of 4. The intersection
   is a single control. The scorer refuses, correctly and loudly.

**This is the engine's gate working.** Nothing was silently averaged over
different populations; the tool said "NOT comparable" and named why. The failure
is in the campaign design, and specifically in mine: I sized S3 four hours
before running it and never asked what a single call costs against the $1 cap
that the same plan document already recorded as not-a-pre-spend-cap (S0-4, where
a `$0.05` cap produced a `$0.098` call). The mechanism was written down; I did
not carry it into the arm that critiques AND adjudicates AND iterates.

## The live resume test, finally answered

S1 completed before an interrupt could be sent. S3 was interrupted after two
cells and resumed. From the resumed ledger:

```
cell.skipped   reason: finalized     <- indep_verify
cell.skipped   reason: finalized     <- indep_verify_iter
cell.started   arm: indep_verify_critique
run.resumed    control-clean-io-r0-indep_verify_critique
stage.resumed  stage: pack
stage.resumed  stage: propose, worker_id: claude
```

Finalized cells skipped by name; the interrupted cell resumed; **completed
stages inside it resumed too** - `pack` and claude's `propose` were neither
re-run nor re-paid. That is what the hermetic test asserts, now confirmed
against real workers and real money. This closes the last open operability
question from S1.

## The quality signal, such as it is

Not a comparison. Descriptions of single arms on 2 plan tasks:

* **`indep_verify` returned ACCEPT on BOTH plan tasks** - `wrong_accept_rate
  1.0` - while covering 4 of the 5 planted findings. It found the problems and
  accepted the document anyway. In S1 the same arm scored 0.5 on the same two
  tasks with a 4-family panel. Two observations at n=2 apiece; the direction is
  not evidence, but the failure mode is legible and worth watching.
* **`indep_verify_iter` covered 5/5** at precision 0.18 - it named every planted
  finding and 23 things that were not there.
* **Controls draw REVISE.** Five of six scored control cells returned REVISE or
  ESCALATE on code with nothing planted; one returned ACCEPT. `wrong_accept`
  cannot see any of that, by construction (S1-D).

## Before anything else runs

1. **Raise or remove the per-worker `max_budget_usd: 1.0`.** It is not a
   pre-spend cap, it aborts a call mid-flight, and on plan-sized documents a
   single critic or adjudicator call exceeds it. Any arm-4 campaign will keep
   losing its plan cells until this is set from measured call costs - claude's
   calls here ran $0.70 average and the aborted ones exceeded $1.
2. **`DISSENT_EMPTY_WITH_CONTEST` must be settled before it is designed
   around.** It is deferred by decision, it fires on real cells, and it drops
   them from every comparison.
3. **Do not re-run S3 as-is.** The stop condition says a budget stop means the
   sizing was wrong; it was, and re-running without fixing (1) buys the same
   blocked comparison a second time.
